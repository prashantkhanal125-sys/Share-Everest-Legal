Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 15, 2026

At Stock Everest ("we", "us", or "our"), we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and your rights when using our application.

1. Information We Collect

To use Stock Everest, you sign in with your Google account. We collect and securely store your email address in Firebase/Firestore to identify your account, manage your subscription, send important notifications, and provide our services.

The account-related information you enter into the app, such as your MeroShare ID, BOID, CRN, passwords, Transaction PIN, and other account details, is stored only on your own device. This information is never uploaded to, stored on, or accessed through our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your email address to authenticate your account, manage your subscription, provide app features, send important notifications, and improve our services. We do not use your personal information for advertising or any purpose unrelated to the operation of the app.

3. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect the personal information stored on our services. Although we work to keep your information secure, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can guarantee complete security.

Your account-related information remains stored only on your own device, and we do not have access to it. If a security issue affecting our services occurs, we will take appropriate action in accordance with applicable laws and platform requirements.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Your email address is retained only for as long as it is necessary to provide our services, manage your subscription, or comply with legal obligations.

If you request deletion of your account, we will remove the personal information stored on our services within a reasonable period, unless we are legally required to retain certain information.

Account-related information stored locally on your device is never retained on our servers. You can remove this information at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the application.

5. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party service providers, including Google Firebase, to support essential features such as user authentication, cloud-based services, notifications, subscriptions, and other app functionality. These providers may process information as necessary to deliver their services and operate under their own privacy policies.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to your personal information, ask us to correct inaccurate information, or request deletion of your account where applicable. If you have any questions or concerns regarding your privacy, you may contact us using the details provided below.

7. App Updates

We may update or improve Stock Everest from time to time by adding new features, improving security, fixing bugs, enhancing performance, or complying with legal and Google Play requirements. Any updates to this Privacy Policy will be published with a revised effective date.

8. Children's Privacy

Stock Everest is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
